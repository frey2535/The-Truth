# War Scroll

**1QM** — The War of the Sons of Light against the Sons of Darkness. Cave 4 copies 4Q491–496. English for reading in this app. Photographs: Leon Levy Digital Library.

## Column 1 — The war

For the Maskil: the rule of the war.

The first attack of the sons of light shall be against the lot of the sons of darkness, against the army of Belial, against the troop of Edom and Moab and the sons of Ammon, against the army of the sons of the East and of Philistia, and against the troops of the Kittim of Assyria, and with them the offenders against the covenant as a help.

The sons of Levi, the sons of Judah, and the sons of Benjamin, the exiles of the wilderness, shall fight against them with […] all their troops, when the exiles of the sons of light return from the wilderness of the peoples to camp in the wilderness of Jerusalem.

After the battle they shall go up from there. In that day, when the Kittim fall, there shall be a battle and great carnage before the God of Israel. For that is a day appointed from of old for a battle of destruction for the sons of darkness. On it the congregation of gods and the assembly of men shall contend in great destruction. The sons of light and the lot of darkness shall fight together for the might of God, with the sound of a great tumult and the war-cry of gods and men, on the day of calamity.

It shall be a time of distress for the people redeemed of God, of all their distresses none like it, from its being until the time of eternal redemption. In that day the sons of light shall fight for a lot of six lots, to strike down the wicked. In the seventh lot the great hand of God shall subdue Belial and all the angels of his dominion, and all the men of his lot.

## Columns 2–3 — The temple service and the trumpets

During the thirty-three remaining years of the war, the men of renown, those called to the assembly, and all the heads of the fathers of the congregation, shall choose for themselves men of war. In the years of release they shall not arm themselves to go out to battle, for they are a sabbath of rest for Israel.

The fathers of the congregation, fifty-two. The chiefs of the priests, they and their deputies, twelve; to serve always before God. The chiefs of the courses, twenty-six. After them the chiefs of the Levites, to serve always, twelve. The chiefs of their courses, twenty-six. The chiefs of the tribes, and the fathers of the congregation after them, to stand always in the gates of the sanctuary.

They shall arrange the chiefs of the priests behind the Chief Priest and his deputy, twelve chiefs to serve in the daily burnt offering before God. The twenty-six chiefs of the courses shall serve in their courses.

On the trumpets of assembly they shall write: “The called of God.” On the trumpets of ambush: “The princes of God.” On the trumpets of pursuit: “God has struck all sons of darkness; he will not turn back his anger until they are destroyed.”

On the trumpets of memorial: “The memorial of vengeance in the appointed time of God.”

When they go to battle they shall write on their standards: “Truth of God,” “Righteousness of God,” “Glory of God,” “Judgment of God,” and after them a whole list of their names.

When they draw near to the battle they shall write: “Right hand of God,” “Appointed time of God,” “Tumult of God,” “Slain of God.” When they return from battle they shall write on their standards: “Exaltations of God,” “Greatness of God,” “Praise of God,” “Glory of God.”

## Columns 5–6 — Weapons and formations

The rule for arranging the battle standards.

When they go to battle they shall make three lines, and they shall go between the lines. They shall send out seven formations of infantry. The formation shall have a standing of a thousand men. Each formation shall have seven front ranks, arranged in order, each man behind the other.

They shall all hold shields of bronze, polished like a face-mirror. The shield shall be bound with a border of woven work, a work of skill, gold and silver and bronze, blended, and precious stones, many-coloured work, the work of a skilful craftsman.

The spear shall be seven cubits long. Of it, the socket and the blade, four and a half cubits. On the socket there shall be three rings, inlaid, gold and silver and bronze, blended, like a work of skill, and a many-coloured design.

The swords shall be of refined iron, the work of a smith, in the manner of a face-mirror, the work of a skilful craftsman, with figures of ears of wheat, of pure gold, set in it, and with many-coloured work on both sides.

Seven formations of horsemen shall also stand at the right and at the left of the line. Their standing shall be seven hundred horsemen on one side and seven hundred on the other. Two hundred horsemen shall go out with the thousand of the line of infantry. So they shall be: six thousand in all — five hundred to a tribe.

All the horsemen who go out to the battle with the infantry shall be from forty to fifty years old. The horsemen of the lines shall be from forty to fifty years old. They and the chiefs of the camps shall be from fifty to sixty years old.

## Columns 7–9 — Age, purity, and the battle

The men of the army shall be from forty to fifty years old. Those who order the camps shall be from fifty to sixty years old. The officers shall be from forty to fifty years old. The despoilers of the slain, the plunderers of spoil, the cleansers of the land, the keepers of the equipment, and those who prepare the food, shall be from twenty-five to thirty years old.

No boy or woman shall enter their camps, from the time they go out of Jerusalem to go to war until they return. No lame, blind, or crippled man, or a man with a permanent blemish in his flesh, or a man stricken with uncleanness in his flesh — none of these shall go with them to battle. All of them shall be volunteers for war, perfect in spirit and in flesh, and ready for the day of vengeance.

Any man who is not clean from his spring on the day of battle shall not go down with them, for the holy angels are with their hosts.

There shall be a space between all their camps and the place of the hand, about two thousand cubits. No unseemly nakedness shall be seen in the surroundings of their camps.

When they set the lines against the enemy, line against line, there shall go out from the middle opening into the space between the lines seven priests of the sons of Aaron, clothed in garments of white linen — a linen tunic and linen trousers, and girded with a linen sash, and mitres on their heads: garments of war. They shall not bring them into the sanctuary.

The first priest shall walk before the men of the line to strengthen their hands in battle. In the seven lots the priests shall sound on the six trumpets of memorial a shrill staccato note, to direct the battle. The Levites and all the people with ram’s horns shall sound a great war-cry, to melt the heart of the enemy.

## Columns 10–14 — Prayers and the high priest’s speech

They shall bless, standing, the God of Israel. They shall answer and say:

“Blessed is the God of Israel, who keeps kindness for his covenant and appointed times of salvation for the people he has redeemed. He has called those who stumble to wondrous mighty deeds. He has gathered an assembly of nations for destruction without remnant. He has raised up in judgment the melting heart, to open the mouth of the dumb to sing of God’s mighty deeds, and to teach war to the weak hands. He gives standing to those who have been smitten, and he girds the shaken knees with strength.

To God belongs the power, and to the heights of greatness the battle is. For our Lord is holy, and the King of Glory is with us, together with the holy ones. The mighty of the host of angels are among our numbered men. The Hero of War is in our congregation, and the host of his spirits is with our steps. Our horsemen are like clouds and like mists covering the earth, and like a rainstorm shedding judgment on all that grows in it.”

The Chief Priest shall take his stand, and his brothers the priests, and the Levites, and all the men of the army with him. He shall bless the God of Israel there, and all his true works. He shall answer and say:

“Blessed is your name, God of gods, for you have done great and wondrous things from of old. You keep the covenant for our fathers, and with all our generations you have shown your mercies to the remnant and the survivors, in the dominion of Belial. In all the mysteries of his hatred they have not led us astray from your covenant.

You, O God of our fathers, we bless your name forever. We are the people of your redemption, and the flock of your pasture.

You from of old appointed for yourself a day of battle […] to help truth and to destroy iniquity, to humble darkness and to make light mighty, and to give to the […] an appointed time of peace, forever.

Today is your appointed time, to humble the prince of the dominion of wickedness. You will send eternal help to the lot you have redeemed, by the power of the angel you have made prince, Michael, in eternal light, to light up with joy the covenant of Israel, peace and blessing for the lot of God, to exalt the authority of Michael among the gods, and the dominion of Israel among all flesh.

Righteousness shall rejoice in the heights, and all the sons of his truth shall shout for joy in eternal knowledge. Blessed are you, God, who in your greatness, in the heights, do a work of greatness.”

## Columns 15–19 — The seven lots

This is the rule for the Chief Priest, when the Kittim fall, and for all the assembly of his service, when they are gathered to go to battle, and for all the congregation, when they come to the slain.

He shall strengthen their hands in the power of God, and their heart in his war. He shall make their fear depart. Afterward he shall number all the formations, as it is written in the Book of the Rule of his Time, for all the works of their wars.

The first lot: the sons of light shall put the lot of Belial to flight. The second lot: the army of Belial shall strengthen themselves to the fall of the lot of light. The third and the fourth: the angels of destruction shall be let loose against all the sons of darkness, and they shall be routed. The fifth and the sixth: the hand of God shall strengthen the heart of the sons of light. The seventh lot: the great hand of God shall subdue Belial and all the angels of his dominion, and all the men of his lot, for an eternal destruction.

On that day the priests shall sound the trumpets of memorial, and the gates of battle shall open. The infantry shall go out and stand in columns between the lines. The priests shall sound for them the trumpets of memorial, and the ram’s horns shall sound the war-cry. The slingers shall throw seven times, and they shall return to their standing.

The priests shall sound on the trumpets of the slain. The battle columns shall spread out against the whole line of the enemy. They shall take hold of him. The sound of the ram’s horns shall be a great war-cry, to melt the heart of the enemy. With the sound of the war-cry, the swords shall fall, to bring down the slain.

The Kittim shall be crushed without remnant or survivor. The king of the Kittim and the army of Belial shall be given into the hand of the poor whom you have redeemed, by the power of your wonderful might.

Then they shall gather in the camp this night to rest until the morning. In the morning they shall come to the place of the line where the mighty of the Kittim fell, and the multitude of Assyria, and the army of all the nations assembled, so that wrath might be spent on […]

The High Priest shall draw near, and his deputy, and the priests, and the Levites […] and the chiefs of the formation […] They shall bless the God of Israel.

“You are the God of our fathers. We bless your name forever. We are the people of your inheritance. You have done a wonder among the peoples, and we have seen it. You have shown us your great and holy wonders from of old. You, O God, have redeemed us for yourself as an eternal people.

Rise up, rise up, O God of gods. Be exalted in your power, King of kings. Put all the sons of darkness in a place of guilt. Let your great light shine […] on gods and on men. Let it burn, the fire of your rebuke, in the dark places of Abaddon. Let it burn in the guilty of Sheol. Let the […] of eternal burning […] in all the sons of darkness. Let them have no remnant.”
