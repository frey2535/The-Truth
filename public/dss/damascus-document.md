# Damascus Document

Known from the Cairo Genizah (CD) and from Qumran copies (4Q266–273, 5Q12, 6Q15). English for reading in this app, after the Cairo recension, with the Qumran order of the laws. Photographs of the cave copies: Leon Levy Digital Library.

## Admonition

Now therefore listen, all you who know righteousness, and understand the works of God. For he has a dispute with all flesh, and he will execute judgment on all who despise him.

When they were unfaithful in forsaking him, he hid his face from Israel and from his sanctuary, and he gave them to the sword. But when he remembered the covenant of the first ones, he left a remnant to Israel and did not give them to destruction.

In the age of wrath, three hundred and ninety years after he had given them into the hand of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, he visited them, and he made to sprout from Israel and from Aaron a root of planting, to inherit his land and to grow fat on the good of his soil.

They understood their iniquity and they knew that they were guilty men. They were like the blind and like those who grope for the way, for twenty years. God considered their deeds, for they sought him with a whole heart, and he raised up for them a Teacher of Righteousness, to lead them in the way of his heart.

He made known to later generations what he had done in a former generation, to a congregation of traitors. They are those who turned aside from the way. This is the time of which it is written: “Like a stubborn heifer, so Israel was stubborn.” When the Man of Mockery arose, who dripped to Israel waters of falsehood and led them astray in a wilderness without a way, to bring down the everlasting heights, to turn aside from the paths of righteousness, and to remove the boundary which the first ones had set in their inheritance — to make cling to them the curses of his covenant, to deliver them to the sword that avenges the vengeance of the covenant.

They sought smooth things. They chose illusions. They watched for breaches. They chose the best of the neck. They justified the wicked and condemned the righteous. They transgressed the covenant and violated the statute. They banded together against the soul of the righteous, and all who walked perfectly they abhorred in their soul. They pursued them with the sword and rejoiced in the strife of the people. So the wrath of God was kindled against their congregation, to make desolate all their multitude, and their deeds were uncleanness before him.

### The two ways

And now listen to me, all who have entered the covenant, and I will uncover your ear to the ways of the wicked.

God loves knowledge. Wisdom and counsel he has set before him. Prudence and knowledge minister to him. Long-suffering is with him, and abundance of pardon, to atone for those who repent of transgression. But power, might, and great wrath with flames of fire, in which are all the angels of destruction, are for those who turned aside from the way and abhorred the statute — so that there should be no remnant or survivor for them.

For God did not choose them from of old. Before they were established he knew their deeds. He abhorred their generations from of old, and he hid his face from the land until they were consumed.

Yet in all of them he raised up for himself men called by name, to leave a remnant for the land, and to fill the face of the world with their seed. Through his Messiah he will make them know his holy spirit. He is true, and in the true interpretation of his name are their names. But those he hated he made to go astray.

### The Watchers and the remnant

And now, children, listen to me, and I will open your eyes to see and to understand the works of God, to choose what he desires and to reject what he hates; to walk perfectly in all his ways, and not to go about in thoughts of a guilty inclination and lustful eyes.

For many have gone astray in them, and mighty men of valour have stumbled in them, from of old until now. In their walking in the stubbornness of their hearts the Watchers of heaven fell. They were caught in it, because they did not keep the commandments of God. Their sons, whose height was like the height of cedars, and whose bodies were like mountains, fell. All flesh that was on dry land perished. They were as though they had not been, because they did their own will and did not keep the commandment of their Maker, until his wrath was kindled against them.

In it the sons of Noah and their families went astray. In it they were cut off. Abraham did not walk in it. He was counted a friend, because he kept the commandments of God and did not choose the will of his own spirit. He passed them on to Isaac and to Jacob. They kept them and were written as friends of God and as members of the covenant forever.

The sons of Jacob went astray in them and were punished according to their error. Their sons in Egypt walked in the stubbornness of their hearts, plotting against the commandments of God, each doing what was right in his own eyes. They ate blood. The men were cut off in the wilderness. At Kadesh he said to them, “Go up and possess the land,” but they chose the desire of their spirit and did not listen to the voice of their Maker, the commandments of their Teacher. They murmured in their tents. The wrath of God was kindled against their congregation.

Their sons perished through it. Their kings were cut off through it. Their mighty men perished through it. Their land became desolate through it. Through it the first ones who entered the covenant were guilty and were given to the sword, because they forsook the covenant of God and chose their own will. They went about after the stubbornness of their heart, each doing his own will.

### The new covenant

But with those who held fast to the commandments of God, who were left of them, God established his covenant with Israel forever, revealing to them the hidden things in which all Israel had gone astray: his holy Sabbaths, his glorious appointed times, his righteous testimonies, his true ways, and the desires of his will, which a man shall do and live by them. He opened before them, and they dug a well of many waters. Those who despise them shall not live.

They defiled themselves in human uncleanness and in ways of uncleanness, and they said, “It is ours.” But God in his wonderful mysteries atoned for their iniquity and pardoned their transgression. He built for them a sure house in Israel, the like of which has not stood from of old until now. Those who hold fast to it are for eternal life, and all the glory of Adam is for them. As God swore to them through Ezekiel the prophet, saying: “The priests and the Levites and the sons of Zadok, who kept the charge of my sanctuary when the children of Israel went astray from me, they shall bring near to me fat and blood.”

The priests are the captives of Israel who went out from the land of Judah. The Levites are those who joined them. The sons of Zadok are the chosen of Israel, the men of renown, who shall stand at the end of days.

Behold, it is all written according to the exact interpretation of their names, for their generations, and the time of their standing, the number of their distresses, the years of their sojourning, and the exact interpretation of their deeds.

### The three nets of Belial

In all those years Belial will be let loose against Israel, as God spoke through Isaiah the prophet, the son of Amoz, saying: “Terror and the pit and the snare are upon you, inhabitant of the land.” Its interpretation: the three nets of Belial, of which Levi the son of Jacob spoke, in which he catches Israel and makes them appear before them as three kinds of righteousness.

The first is fornication. The second is wealth. The third is defilement of the sanctuary. He who escapes from one is caught in the next. He who is saved from that is caught in the next.

The builders of the wall who walk after the “command” — the “command” is a spouter, of whom he said, “They shall surely spout” — they are caught in two: in fornication, by taking two wives in their lifetime, whereas the foundation of creation is “male and female he created them,” and those who went into the ark “went two by two into the ark.” And concerning the prince it is written: “He shall not multiply wives for himself.” David did not read in the sealed book of the Torah which was in the ark, for it was not opened in Israel from the day of the death of Eleazar and Joshua and the elders who served the Ashtaroth. It was hidden and not revealed until Zadok arose. The deeds of David rose up, except the blood of Uriah, and God left them to him.

They also defile the sanctuary, for they do not separate according to the Torah, and they lie with her who sees the blood of her flux. They take each the daughter of his brother and the daughter of his sister.

But all who are brought into the covenant, not to enter the sanctuary to kindle his altar in vain, shall be those who close the door, of whom God said, “Who among you will close my door, and you shall not kindle my altar in vain?” Unless they take care to do according to the exact interpretation of the Torah for the time of wickedness, to separate from the sons of the pit, to keep away from unrighteous wealth which is unclean by vow and devoted thing, and from the wealth of the sanctuary, and to rob the poor of his people, making widows their spoil, and murdering orphans; to distinguish between unclean and clean, and to make known between holy and profane; to keep the Sabbath day according to its exact interpretation, and the appointed times, and the day of the fast, according to the commandment of those who entered the new covenant in the land of Damascus; to set aside the holy things according to their exact interpretation; for a man to love his brother as himself; to strengthen the hand of the poor and the needy and the sojourner; to seek each the well-being of his brother; for a man not to trespass against his next of kin; to keep away from fornication according to the statute; to rebuke each his brother according to the commandment, and not to keep a grudge from day to day; to separate from all kinds of uncleanness according to their statutes; for a man not to defile his holy spirit, according as God distinguished for them.

All who walk in these in perfect holiness, according to all the instruction of the covenant of God — the covenant of God stands firm for them, to make them live a thousand generations. As it is written: “He keeps covenant and kindness to those who love him and to those who keep his commandments, to a thousand generations.”

### The well

The well which the princes dug, which the nobles of the people delved with the staff — the well is the Torah. Those who dug it are the captives of Israel, who went out from the land of Judah and sojourned in the land of Damascus. God called them all princes, for they sought him, and their glory was not rejected by any mouth. The staff is the Interpreter of the Torah, of whom Isaiah said, “He brings forth a tool for his work.” The nobles of the people are those who come to dig the well with the staffs which the Staff decreed, to walk in them throughout the whole age of wickedness. Without them they shall not succeed, until there arises he who teaches righteousness at the end of days.

None of those who have entered the covenant and have broken the bound of the Torah shall be counted in the council of the people or written in their writing, from the day of the gathering in of the Unique Teacher until there arises the Messiah from Aaron and from Israel.

Such is the judgment for all who enter the congregation of the men of perfect holiness. Any man among them who is not pleased to rebuke, and to do according to the will of God, and who betrays the uprightness of his teaching, he is the man who is melted in the midst of a furnace. When his deeds become apparent, he shall be sent away from the congregation as one whose lot has not fallen in the midst of the taught of God. According to his trespass the men of knowledge shall rebuke him, until the day when he returns to stand in the session of the men of perfect holiness.

When his deeds become apparent, according to the interpretation of the Torah in which the men of perfect holiness walk, let no man agree with him in wealth or in work. For all the holy ones of the Most High have cursed him.

Such is also the judgment for every one who rejects the first and the last, who have put idols on their heart and have walked in the stubbornness of their heart. They have no share in the house of the Torah.

They shall be judged according to the judgment of their companions who turned aside with the men of mockery. For they spoke error against the statutes of righteousness, and they despised the covenant and the pledge which they had affirmed in the land of Damascus. This is the new covenant. Neither they nor their families shall have a share in the house of the Torah.

From the day of the gathering in of the Unique Teacher until the end of all the men of war who turned back with the Man of the Lie, there will be about forty years. In that age the wrath of God will be kindled against Israel, as he said, “There is no king and no prince and no judge, and none who rebukes in righteousness.” Those who turned from the way of the people, on the basis of the teaching of the Unique One, will be written according to their names, each man according to his spirit, in the lot of light, with all the reward of his deeds.

### The two houses

In the age of the desolation of the land there arose the movers of the bound, and they led Israel astray. The land became desolate, for they spoke rebellion against the commandments of God through Moses, and also through the holy anointed ones. They prophesied falsehood, to turn Israel away from God.

But God remembered the covenant of the first ones. He raised from Aaron men of understanding, and from Israel wise men. He made them listen, and they dug the well. “A well the princes dug, the nobles of the people delved with the staff.”

The well is the Torah. Those who dug it are the captives of Israel, who went out from the land of Judah and sojourned in the land of Damascus. God called them all princes. The staff is the Interpreter of the Torah.

The star is the Interpreter of the Torah who came to Damascus, as it is written: “A star has come forth from Jacob, and a scepter has arisen from Israel.” The scepter is the prince of the whole congregation. When he arises, he will destroy all the sons of Seth.

These escaped in the time of the first visitation. But those who turned back were given to the sword. Such will be the judgment of all who enter his covenant, who do not hold fast to these statutes: they will be visited for destruction by the hand of Belial. That is the day when God will visit.

The princes of Judah are those of whom he poured out wrath. They will be moved. They are sick. The hope of their healing is in vain. They are all rebels, because they have not turned from the way of traitors, and they have defiled themselves in ways of fornication and in unrighteous wealth, and in taking revenge and bearing a grudge, each against his brother, and hating each his neighbour. They have sinned, each against the flesh of his next of kin, and they have drawn near for incest. They have made themselves mighty for the sake of wealth and unjust gain. Each did what was right in his eyes, and each chose the stubbornness of his heart. They did not separate from the people, and they have rebelled with a high hand, walking in the way of the wicked, of whom God said, “Their wine is the poison of serpents, the cruel venom of asps.” The serpents are the kings of the peoples, and their wine is their ways, and the venom of asps is the head of the kings of Greece, who comes to do vengeance on them.

But all who hold fast to these rules, going out and coming in according to the Torah, and who listen to the voice of the Teacher, and confess before God: “We have sinned, we have been wicked, we and our fathers, in walking contrary to the statutes of the covenant. Righteousness and truth are yours” — God will atone for them, and they will see his salvation, for they have taken refuge in his holy name.

## Laws

### The Sabbath

Concerning the Sabbath, to keep it according to its exact interpretation:

Let no man do work on the sixth day from the time when the disk of the sun is distant from the gate by its fullness. For that is what he said: “Keep the Sabbath day to sanctify it.”

On the Sabbath day let no man speak a foolish or empty word. Let him not lend anything to his neighbour. Let him not dispute about wealth or gain. Let him not speak of matters of work or of labour to be done on the next morning.

Let a man not walk in the field to do the work of his desire on the Sabbath. Let him not walk outside his city more than a thousand cubits.

Let him not eat on the Sabbath day except what has been prepared. Let him not eat what is lost in the field. Let him not drink except what is in the camp.

If he is on a journey and goes down to wash, let him drink where he stands, but let him not draw into any vessel.

Let him not send a foreigner to do his desire on the Sabbath day.

Let a man not put on soiled garments, or garments brought with money, unless they are washed in water or rubbed with frankincense.

Let no man go after a beast to pasture it outside his city, except two thousand cubits.

Let him not raise his hand to strike it with a fist. If it is stubborn, let him not bring it out of his house.

Let a man not take anything out of the house, or bring anything in, on the Sabbath. Let him not open a sealed vessel on the Sabbath.

Let a man not carry spices on him, going out or coming in, on the Sabbath. Let him not lift in his dwelling a stone or dust.

Let a nurse not carry a child to go out or come in on the Sabbath.

Let a man not provoke his servant or his maidservant or his hired man on the Sabbath.

Let a man not help a beast to give birth on the Sabbath day. If it falls into a pit or a ditch, let him not raise it on the Sabbath.

Let a man not spend the Sabbath in a place near to the gentiles on the Sabbath.

Let a man not profane the Sabbath for the sake of wealth or of gain on the Sabbath.

Any living man who falls into a place of water or a place of […] let no man bring him up with a ladder or a rope or a vessel.

Let a man not bring up anything to the altar on the Sabbath, except the burnt offering of the Sabbath, for thus it is written: “Except your Sabbaths.”

Let a man not send to the altar a burnt offering or a grain offering or frankincense or wood by the hand of a man who is unclean with any uncleanness, giving him to defile the altar. For it is written: “The sacrifice of the wicked is an abomination, but the prayer of the righteous is like an acceptable offering.”

### The camp, the Examiner, and the Many

This is the rule for the session of the camps.

Those who walk in these in the time of wickedness until the arising of the Messiah of Aaron and of Israel shall be as ten men at least, by thousands and hundreds and fifties and tens. When ten are there, a priest learned in the Book of Meditation shall not be lacking. All shall be ruled according to his word.

If he is not expert in all these, but a man of the Levites is expert, the lot shall be that all who enter the camp shall go out and come in according to his word.

This is the rule of the Examiner of the camp. He shall instruct the Many in the works of God, and make them understand his wonderful mighty deeds. He shall recount before them the things of the world from of old. He shall have mercy upon them as a father upon his sons, and he shall forgive all who have incurred guilt. As a shepherd with his flock he shall loose all the bonds of their knots.

Everyone who joins his congregation, he shall examine him as to his deeds, his understanding, his strength, his might, and his wealth. They shall register him in his place according to his standing in the lot of the camp.

No man of the children of the camp shall have power to bring a man into the congregation without the word of the Examiner of the camp.

They shall be numbered all by their names: the priests first, the Levites second, the children of Israel third, and the sojourner fourth. Thus they shall sit, and thus they shall be asked, in every matter.

The Examiner who is over all the camps shall be from thirty years old to fifty years old, master in every counsel of men. According to his word shall come in those who enter the congregation, each in his order.

The wages of two days every month shall be given into the hand of the Examiner and the judges. From it they shall strengthen the hand of the poor and the needy, the aged, the vagrant, him who was taken captive of a strange people, the virgin who has no one to seek her, and him for whom no man cares.

### Entry into the covenant

He who enters the covenant, for all Israel for an everlasting statute, together with their children who are not of age to pass over into the number of those enrolled by the oath of the covenant, shall confirm it on their behalf.

On the day when he speaks with the Examiner of the Many they shall enrol him with the oath of the covenant which Moses established with Israel, the covenant to return to the Torah of Moses with all his heart and with all his soul.

No man shall make known to him the judgments until he stands before the Examiner, who shall search him when he examines him.

On the day when a man takes it upon himself to return to the Torah of Moses, the angel of Mastema will depart from him, if he makes good his word. Therefore Abraham was circumcised on the day of his knowing it.

As for the exact statement of their times, to put Israel in remembrance concerning all these, behold, it is treated accurately in the Book of the Divisions of the Times, according to their jubilees and their weeks.
