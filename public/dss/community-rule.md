# Community Rule (Serekh ha-Yahad)

**1QS**, with Cave 4 copies 4Q255–264 and 5Q11. One of the original seven Cave 1 scrolls. English for reading in this app; photographs: Leon Levy Digital Library.

## Column 1 — The covenant

To the Maskil: for his life, the book of the rule of the community.

To seek God with all the heart and with all the soul; to do what is good and right before him, as he commanded through Moses and through all his servants the prophets; to love all that he has chosen and to hate all that he has rejected; to be far from all evil and to cling to all good works; to do truth and righteousness and justice in the land; to walk no longer in the stubbornness of a guilty heart and of lustful eyes, doing all evil.

To bring in all who offer themselves to do the statutes of God into the covenant of kindness; to be united in the counsel of God; to walk before him perfectly, according to all that is revealed for the appointed times of their testimonies; to love all the sons of light, each according to his lot in the counsel of God; and to hate all the sons of darkness, each according to his guilt in the vengeance of God.

All who offer themselves to his truth shall bring all their knowledge, their strength, and their property into the community of God, to purify their knowledge in the truth of the statutes of God, to order their strength according to the perfection of his ways, and all their property according to his righteous counsel.

They shall not depart from any one of all the words of God in their times; they shall not advance their times nor delay any of their appointed seasons. They shall not turn aside from the statutes of his truth, going to the right or to the left.

All who come into the rule of the community shall cross over into the covenant before God, to do according to all that he has commanded, and not to turn back from him for any fear or terror or testing in the dominion of Belial.

When they cross over into the covenant, the priests and the Levites shall bless the God of salvation and all his works of truth. All who cross over into the covenant shall say after them: “Amen, amen.”

The priests shall recount the righteous acts of God in his mighty works, and they shall proclaim all his mercies of kindness toward Israel. The Levites shall recount the iniquities of the children of Israel, and all their guilty transgressions, and their sin in the dominion of Belial.

All who cross over into the covenant shall confess after them: “We have been wicked, we have rebelled, we have sinned, we have done wickedly, we and our fathers before us, in walking contrary to the statutes of truth. Righteousness is yours, and the stain is ours, you and your people.”

## Column 2 — Blessings, curses, and the lot

The priests shall bless all the men of the lot of God who walk perfectly in all his ways, and they shall say:

“May he bless you with all good and keep you from all evil. May he delight your heart with saving knowledge, and may he be gracious to you with everlasting wisdom. May he lift up his merciful face toward you for eternal peace.”

The Levites shall curse all the men of the lot of Belial. They shall answer and say:

“Cursed are you in all your guilty works of wickedness. May God make you an object of dread through all who take vengeance, and may he send after you destruction through all who pay recompense. Cursed are you without mercy, according to the darkness of your deeds. Damned are you in the gloom of everlasting fire. May God not be gracious to you when you call, and may he not forgive by covering your iniquity. May he lift up his angry face for vengeance upon you, and may there be no peace for you in the mouth of those who hold fast the fathers.”

All who cross over into the covenant shall say after those who bless and those who curse: “Amen, amen.”

The priests and the Levites shall continue and say:

“Cursed because of the idols of his heart which he worships is the one who crosses into this covenant while setting the stumbling-block of his iniquity before himself, to turn back in the stubbornness of his heart. He shall be cut off from the midst of all the sons of light, because he turned aside from God for the sake of his idols and the stumbling-block of his iniquity. His lot shall be among those who are cursed forever.”

All who enter the covenant shall answer and say after them: “Amen, amen.”

Thus they shall do, year by year, all the days of the dominion of Belial.

The priests shall cross over first in the order, according to their spirits, one after another. The Levites shall cross after them. All the people shall cross third in order, one after another, by thousands and hundreds and fifties and tens, so that every man of Israel may know his standing in the community of God, for an eternal council. No man shall be made low from his standing, nor raised from the place of his lot. For they shall all be in a community of truth, virtuous humility, love of kindness, and right purpose — each toward his neighbour in the holy council, sons of an everlasting assembly.

Whoever refuses to enter the covenant of God, walking in the stubbornness of his heart, shall not enter the community of his truth. For his soul has abhorred the disciplines of knowledge of righteous judgments. He has not held fast to convert his life. He shall not be counted with the upright. His knowledge, his strength, and his property shall not come into the council of the community. For in the mire of wickedness is his ploughing, and defilement is in his conversion. He shall not be justified while he clings to the stubbornness of his heart. Darkness he looks upon as the ways of light. In the spring of the perfect he shall not be counted. He shall not be cleansed by atonements, nor made holy by seas or rivers, nor purified by any water of washing. Unclean, unclean he shall be, as long as he rejects the judgments of God, so that he is not instructed in the community of his counsel.

For by the spirit of the true counsel of God the ways of a man are atoned, all his iniquities, to look on the light of life. By a holy spirit, united in his truth, he is cleansed from all his iniquities. By an upright and humble spirit his sin is atoned. When his flesh is sprinkled with water of purification and made holy by water of washing, it is by the submission of his soul to all the statutes of God that his flesh is cleansed — to walk perfectly in all the ways of God, as he commanded for the appointed times of his testimonies, and not to turn aside to right or left, and not to transgress a one of all his words. Then he will be accepted with soothing offerings before God, and it will be to him a covenant of the everlasting community.

## Columns 3–4 — The two spirits

The Maskil shall instruct and teach all the sons of light in the history of all the sons of man, concerning all the kinds of their spirits, with their signs, for their deeds in their generations, and for the visitation of their afflictions together with the times of their peace.

From the God of knowledge comes all that is and that will be. Before they came to be, he established all their design. When they come to be at their appointed times, they fulfill their task according to his glorious design. Nothing can be changed. In his hand are the judgments of all, and he provides them in all their needs.

He created man to rule the world, and he appointed for him two spirits in which to walk until the time of his visitation: the spirits of truth and of injustice.

From a spring of light is the generation of truth, and from a well of darkness is the generation of injustice. In the hand of the Prince of Lights is the dominion of all the sons of righteousness; in the ways of light they walk. In the hand of the Angel of Darkness is all the dominion of the sons of injustice; in the ways of darkness they walk. By the Angel of Darkness comes the straying of all the sons of righteousness. All their sin, their iniquities, their guilt, and the transgressions of their deeds are in his dominion, according to the mysteries of God, until his time. All their afflictions and the appointed times of their distress are in the dominion of his hostility. All the spirits of his lot seek to make the sons of light stumble. But the God of Israel and the angel of his truth help all the sons of light.

He created the spirits of light and of darkness, and upon them he founded every work, upon their ways every labour. One God loves for all the appointed times of the world, and in all its deeds he delights forever. The other he abhors, its counsel and all its ways he hates forever.

These are their ways in the world. To the spirit of truth belong: enlightenment of the heart, and the straightness of the paths of righteousness; to make a man fear the judgments of God; a spirit of humility, patience, abundant mercy, eternal goodness, understanding, insight, mighty wisdom trusting in all the deeds of God and leaning on the abundance of his kindness; a spirit of knowledge in every plan of action, zeal for the judgments of righteousness, holy purpose with firm inclination, abundant kindness toward all the sons of truth, glorious purity abhorring all the idols of uncleanness, walking with reserve in wisdom concerning all things, and concealing the truth of the mysteries of knowledge.

These are the counsels of the spirit for the sons of truth in the world. The visitation of all who walk in it will be healing, and abundant peace in length of days, fruitfulness of seed with all everlasting blessings, and eternal joy in the life of eternity, and a crown of glory with a robe of honour in eternal light.

To the spirit of injustice belong: greed, slackness of hands in the service of righteousness, wickedness, falsehood, pride, haughtiness of heart, lying, deceit, cruelty, much hypocrisy, impatience, much folly, zeal for insolence, works of abomination in a spirit of fornication, ways of uncleanness in the service of impurity, a blaspheming tongue, blindness of eyes, dullness of ears, stiffness of neck, hardness of heart — walking in all the ways of darkness, and evil cunning.

The visitation of all who walk in it will be a multitude of afflictions at the hand of all the angels of destruction, eternal ruin by the fury of the wrath of the God of vengeance, everlasting terror and perpetual shame, with the disgrace of destruction in the fire of dark places. All their times, for their generations, will be in sorrowful mourning and bitter distress, in dark disasters, until they are destroyed, without remnant or survivor.

In these (two) are the histories of all the sons of man. In their divisions all their hosts inherit, for their generations. In their ways they walk. All the work of their deeds is in their divisions, according to a man’s share, much or little, in all the appointed times of eternity. For God has set them in equal measure until the last time, and he has put eternal enmity between their divisions. An abomination to truth are the deeds of injustice, and an abomination to injustice are all the ways of truth. A jealous struggle is over all their judgments, for they do not walk together.

God, in the mysteries of his understanding and in his glorious wisdom, has appointed an end for the existence of injustice. At the appointed time of visitation he will destroy it forever. Then the truth of the world will appear forever. For it has wallowed in the ways of wickedness in the dominion of injustice, until the appointed time of judgment which has been determined. Then God will purge by his truth all the works of a man, and refine for himself the sons of man. He will destroy every spirit of injustice from the inward parts of his flesh, and cleanse him by a holy spirit from all wicked deeds. He will sprinkle upon him a spirit of truth, like water of purification, from all abominations of falsehood and wallowing in a spirit of uncleanness — to instruct the upright in the knowledge of the Most High, and the wisdom of the sons of heaven to the perfect of way. For God has chosen them for an eternal covenant, and to them belongs all the glory of Adam. There will be no more injustice. All the works of deceit will be a shame.

Until now the spirits of truth and of injustice struggle in the heart of a man. They walk in wisdom and in folly. According to a man’s inheritance in truth he is righteous, and so he hates injustice. According to his share in the lot of injustice he does wickedly in it, and so he abhors truth. For in equal measure God has set them until the determined end and the making of new things. He knows the work of their deeds for all the appointed times of eternity, and he has given them as an inheritance to the sons of man, for knowledge of good and to give the inheritance to every living thing, according to his spirit — until the time of visitation.

## Columns 5–7 — Rule of the community

This is the rule for the men of the community who offer themselves to turn from all evil and to hold fast to all that he has commanded according to his will: to separate from the congregation of the men of injustice, to be a community in Torah and in property, answering according to the sons of Zadok, the priests who keep the covenant, and according to the multitude of the men of the community, who hold fast to the covenant. On their word the lot shall go forth for every matter of Torah, property, and judgment, to do truth together, and humility, righteousness and justice, love of kindness, and walking discreetly in all their ways.

Let no man walk in the stubbornness of his heart, to stray after his heart and his eyes and the thought of his inclination. They shall circumcise in the community the foreskin of inclination and a stiff neck, to lay a foundation of truth for Israel, for a community of an eternal covenant; to atone for all who offer themselves for holiness in Aaron and for a house of truth in Israel, and for those who join them for community; and a lawsuit and judgment to condemn all who transgress the statute.

They shall do these things according to all the rules, when they are gathered. Whoever enters the council of the community shall enter the covenant of God in the presence of all who offer themselves. He shall take it upon his soul by a binding oath to return to the Torah of Moses, according to all that he commanded, with all his heart and with all his soul, to all that is revealed of it to the sons of Zadok, the priests who keep the covenant and seek his will, and to the multitude of the men of their covenant, who offer themselves together for his truth and to walk in his will.

He shall take it upon his soul by the covenant to separate from all the men of injustice who walk in the way of wickedness. For they are not reckoned in his covenant, for they have not sought or inquired after his statutes to know the hidden things in which they have wandered to their guilt; and the revealed things they have treated with a high hand, raising anger for judgment and for the vengeance of the curses of the covenant, to do in them great judgments for eternal destruction without remnant.

He shall not enter the water to touch the purity of the men of holiness, for they will not be cleansed unless they turn from their wickedness; for uncleanness is in all who transgress his word.

He shall not be united with him in his work or in his property, lest he make him bear guilt. He shall keep far from him in every matter. For thus it is written: “From every false matter you shall keep far.” No man of the men of the community shall answer according to their word in any Torah or judgment.

He shall not eat of any of their property, and he shall not drink, and he shall not take anything from their hand, except for a price. As it is written: “Cease from man, whose breath is in his nostrils, for of what account is he?” For all who are not reckoned in his covenant are to be separated, they and all that is theirs. The man of holiness shall not rely on any works of vanity. For vanity are all who do not know his covenant. All who despise his word he will destroy from the world. All their deeds are uncleanness before him, and uncleanness is in all their property.

When he enters the covenant to do according to all these statutes, to be united with the congregation of holiness, they shall examine his spirit in community, between a man and his neighbour, according to his understanding and his deeds in Torah, according to the word of the sons of Aaron, who offer themselves in community to establish his covenant and to oversee all his statutes which he commanded to do, and according to the word of the multitude of Israel, who offer themselves to return in community to his covenant.

They shall register them in order, one before another, according to his understanding and his deeds, so that every man may obey his neighbour, the lesser the greater. They shall examine their spirits and their deeds year by year, to promote a man according to his understanding and the perfection of his way, or to set him back according to his perversions.

They shall rebuke one another in truth, humility, and love of kindness to a man. Let him not speak to him with anger or with complaint or with a stiff neck or with an envious spirit of wickedness. Let him not hate him in his uncircumcised heart. On that day he shall rebuke him, and not bear iniquity because of him. Also, let no man bring a case against his neighbour before the Many without proof before witnesses.

In these they shall walk in all their dwelling-places, all that is found, each with his neighbour. The lesser shall obey the greater in work and in money. Together they shall eat, together they shall bless, together they shall take counsel.

In every place where there are ten men of the council of the community, there shall not be lacking among them a priest. Each according to his rank, they shall sit before him. Thus they shall be asked for their counsel in every matter.

When the table is prepared for eating, or the new wine for drinking, the priest shall stretch out his hand first to bless the first of the bread and the new wine.

In the place where there are ten, there shall not be lacking a man who studies the Torah day and night, continually, each man relieving another. The Many shall watch together a third of all the nights of the year, to read in the Book, to seek judgment, and to bless together.

This is the rule for the session of the Many. Each in his order: the priests first, the elders second, and the rest of all the people each in his order. Thus they shall be asked concerning judgment and every counsel and matter which is the Many’s, for each to answer his knowledge to the council of the community.

Let no man speak in the midst of his neighbour’s words, before his brother has finished speaking. Also, let him not speak before his rank which is written before him. The man who is asked shall speak in his turn. In a session of the Many, let no man speak any word which is not to the liking of the Many, and if the man who is the Examiner over the Many, and any man who has something to say to the Many, who is not in the standing of the man who is asking the counsel of the community — that man shall stand on his feet and say, “I have something to say to the Many.” If they say to him, “Speak,” he shall speak.

Every man of Israel who offers himself to join the council of the community, the man who is the Examiner at the head of the Many shall examine him concerning his understanding and his deeds. If he is fitted for discipline, he shall bring him into the covenant, to turn to the truth and to turn aside from all injustice. He shall instruct him in all the judgments of the community.

Afterward, when he comes to stand before the Many, they shall all be asked concerning his affairs. As the lot goes forth according to the counsel of the Many, he shall draw near or depart. When he draws near to the council of the community, he shall not touch the purity of the Many until they examine him concerning his spirit and his deeds, until he has completed a full year. Also, he shall not share in the property of the Many.

When he has completed a year in the midst of the community, the Many shall be asked concerning his affairs, according to his understanding and his deeds in Torah. If the lot goes forth for him to draw near to the assembly of the community, according to the priests and the multitude of the men of their covenant, they shall register also his property and his work in the hand of the man who is the Examiner over the work of the Many. It shall be written in the account in his hand; he shall not spend it for the Many.

He shall not touch the drink of the Many until he has completed a second year among the men of the community. When the second year is complete, he shall be examined according to the Many. If the lot goes forth for him to draw near to the community, they shall register him in the order of his standing among his brothers, for Torah and for judgment and for purity, and to mix his property. His counsel and his judgment shall belong to the community.

These are the judgments by which they shall judge in an inquiry of the community, according to the cases.

If there is found among them a man who lies in the matter of property, knowingly, they shall set him back from the purity of the Many for one year, and he shall be fined a fourth of his bread.

Whoever answers his neighbour with a stiff neck, or speaks with impatience, undermining the foundation of his fellow who is registered before him, has taken the law into his own hand. He shall be fined one year.

Whoever mentions anything in the Name which is honoured above all […] whether cursing, or being caught in a sudden accident, or for any other reason, or reading in a book, or blessing — he shall be set apart and shall not return again to the council of the community.

If he has spoken in anger against one of the priests registered in the book, he shall be fined one year and set back, by himself, from the purity of the Many. Afterward he shall return.

If he spoke in inadvertence, he shall be fined six months.

The man who lies knowingly shall be fined six months.

The man who bears a grudge against his neighbour without cause shall be fined six months / one year.

Whoever speaks with his mouth a foolish word: three months.

Whoever interrupts his neighbour’s words: ten days.

Whoever lies down and sleeps in a session of the Many: thirty days.

The man who goes out in a session of the Many without counsel, and without cause, up to three times in one session, shall be fined ten days. If they stand and he goes out, he shall be fined thirty days.

Whoever walks naked before his neighbour, without being forced, shall be fined six months.

A man who spits into the midst of a session of the Many shall be fined thirty days.

Whoever brings out his hand from under his garment, and it is torn, so that his nakedness is seen, shall be fined thirty days.

Whoever laughs foolishly, making his voice heard, shall be fined thirty days.

Whoever brings out his left hand to gesticulate with it shall be fined ten days.

The man who goes about slandering his neighbour shall be set apart from the purity of the Many for one year, and fined. The man who goes about slandering the Many shall be sent away from them, and shall not come back again.

The man who murmurs against the foundation of the community shall be sent away and shall not come back. If he murmurs against his neighbour without cause, he shall be fined six months.

The man whose spirit wavers from the foundation of the community, to betray the truth and to walk in the stubbornness of his heart: if he returns, he shall be fined two years. In the first he shall not touch the purity of the Many. In the second he shall not touch the drink of the Many. After he has completed two years, the Many shall be asked concerning his affairs. If they draw him near, he shall be registered in his standing, and afterward he shall be asked for judgment.

Any man who has been in the council of the community for as long as ten years, and whose spirit turns back to betray the community and to go out from before the Many to walk in the stubbornness of his heart, shall not come back again to the council of the community. A man of the men of the community who mixes with him in his purity or in his property which […] the Many, his judgment shall be like his: he shall be sent away.

## Columns 8–9 — The council of holiness

In the council of the community there shall be twelve men and three priests, perfect in all that is revealed from the whole Torah, to do truth and righteousness and justice and love of kindness and walking discreetly, each with his neighbour; to keep faithfulness in the land with a firm inclination and a broken spirit; to atone for iniquity by doing judgment and the anguish of testing, and to walk with all in the measure of truth and the order of time.

When these things come to pass in Israel, the council of the community shall be established in truth, as an eternal plant, a house of holiness for Israel, a most holy assembly for Aaron; witnesses of truth for judgment, and chosen of good will to atone for the land and to pay the wicked their reward.

It shall be a tested wall, a precious cornerstone. Its foundations shall not shake or move from their place. It shall be a most holy dwelling for Aaron, with everlasting knowledge of the covenant of justice, and to offer a soothing odour; and a house of perfection and truth in Israel, to establish a covenant for everlasting statutes.

They shall be accepted to atone for the land and to decide the judgment of wickedness, and there shall be no more injustice. When these are established in the foundation of the community for two years, in perfection of way, they shall be set apart, holy in the midst of the council of the men of the community. Anything which was hidden from Israel, and is found by a man who seeks, he shall not conceal it from these, from fear of an apostate spirit.

When these become a community in Israel, according to these rules, they shall separate from the midst of the dwelling of the men of injustice, to go to the wilderness to prepare there the way of Him, as it is written: “In the wilderness prepare the way of YHWH; make straight in the desert a highway for our God.” This is the study of the Torah, which he commanded through Moses, to do according to all that is revealed from time to time, and as the prophets have revealed by his holy spirit.

Any man of the men of the community, of the covenant of the community, who turns aside from any commandment with a high hand shall not touch the purity of the men of holiness, and shall not know any of their counsel, until his deeds are purified from all injustice, to walk in perfection of way. They shall draw him near by the decision of the Many, and afterward he shall be registered in his standing.

This is the rule for the men of the community who offer themselves to be a holy thing in Aaron, a house of holiness in Israel, who walk in perfection as he commanded.

Any matter which is hidden from Israel, and is found by a man who studies, he shall not conceal it from these, from fear of a backsliding spirit.

When these exist as a community in Israel according to these rules, they shall separate from the midst of the session of the men of injustice to go to the wilderness to prepare there the way of truth.

These are the statutes for the Maskil, to walk in them with every living being, according to the order of every time and the weight of every man.

He shall do the will of God according to all that is revealed from time to time. He shall learn all the understanding which has been found according to the times, and the statute of the time.

He shall separate and weigh the sons of righteousness according to their spirits. He shall hold fast to the chosen of the time, according to his will, as he has commanded. He shall judge a man according to his spirit. He shall draw near a man according to the cleanness of his hands, and according to his understanding he shall advance him. Thus shall be his love and his hatred.

He shall not rebuke or get into an argument with the men of the pit, but he shall conceal the counsel of the Torah in the midst of the men of injustice. He shall rebuke with true knowledge and righteous judgment those who choose the way, each according to his spirit, according to the order of the time.

He shall lead them in knowledge, and thus instruct them in the wondrous mysteries and truth in the midst of the men of the community, to walk perfectly, a man with his neighbour, in all that is revealed to them. This is the time to prepare the way to the wilderness. He shall instruct them in all that is found to be done in this time, and to be separated from every man who has not turned his way from all injustice.

These are the rules of the way for the Maskil in these times, concerning his love and his hatred: everlasting hatred toward the men of the pit, in a spirit of concealment, leaving to them property and labour of the hands, as a slave does to the one who rules him, and humility before the one who lords it over him.

He shall be a man zealous for the statute and its time, for the day of vengeance. He shall do the will of God in every task of his hands, and in all his dominion as he has commanded. All that is done, he shall delight in willingly. Apart from the will of God he shall not delight.

He shall delight in all the words of his mouth, and shall not desire anything that he has not commanded. He shall watch always for the judgment of God.

In every test he shall praise his Maker, and in every distress he shall recount his wonders. He shall bless him with the offering of the lips.

## Columns 10–11 — Hymn of the Maskil

With the coming of day and night I will enter the covenant of God, and with the outgoing of evening and morning I will speak his statutes. In their being I will set my bound, so as not to turn back.

I will bless him for his wondrous work, at the beginning of fear, in the dwelling of distress, in all that is, to the end.

When I stretch out my hand or my foot, I will bless his name. When I go out or come in, when I sit or rise, and when I lie on my couch, I will cry out to him. I will bless him with the offering of the utterance of my lips, in the row of men.

Before I lift my hands to be filled with the delights of the produce of the earth, at the beginning of fear and dread, and in the dwelling of distress and desolation, I will bless him for his wonder exceedingly. I will meditate on his power, and on his kindness I will lean all the day.

I know that in his hand is the judgment of every living thing, and all his deeds are truth. When distress is let loose I will praise him, and I will rejoice in his salvation as well.

I will not return to a man evil for evil. With good I will pursue a man. For with God is the judgment of every living thing, and he will pay a man his reward.

I will not be jealous in a spirit of wickedness. My anger I will not keep for wealth of violence. I will not seize the men of the pit until the day of vengeance. I will not turn back my anger from men of injustice, and I will not be satisfied until he establishes judgment.

I will not keep Belial in my heart. Folly shall not be heard from my mouth, guilty deceit, or lies, or deceits. Fruit of holiness shall be on my tongue. Abominations shall not be found on it.

With thanksgivings I will open my mouth. The righteous acts of God shall my tongue recount always, and the unfaithfulness of men, until their transgression is complete.

I will make to cease from my lips worthless words, uncleanness and craft from the knowledge of my heart. With wise counsel I will hide knowledge, and with prudent knowledge I will hedge it with a firm bound, to keep faithfulness and strong judgment of the righteousness of God.

I will measure with the measuring-line of the times, and […] righteousness. A statute of the time […] righteousness. Lovingkindness of kindness to the lowly, and strengthening of hands for the anxious of heart.

To the erring of spirit, understanding; to teach those who murmur the lesson; to answer the humble with a broken spirit, and men of injustice with a bridled spirit; to walk with all in the measure of truth, and the order of time.

I will look to him for salvation, and I will wait for his kindness. I have hoped for his atonement. I have leaned on his abundant mercies, and I have hoped for his kindness.

For I know that in his hand is the judgment of every living thing, and all his deeds are truth. When he lets loose his hand, I will praise him. For his salvation I will rejoice.

As for me, my justification is with God. In his hand is the perfection of my way, with the uprightness of my heart. In his righteousness my transgression is wiped out.

For from the spring of his knowledge he has opened my light. My eye has gazed on his wonders, and the light of my heart on the mystery of what is.

The One Who Is is the support of my right hand. The way of my steps is over firm rock. It will not shake because of anything. For the truth of God is the rock of my steps, and his strength is the support of my right hand.

From the spring of his righteousness is my justification. The light in my heart is from his wondrous mysteries. My eye has gazed on what is forever: wisdom which is hidden from man, knowledge and prudent device hidden from the sons of man, a spring of righteousness, a well of strength, and a spring of glory hidden from the assembly of flesh.

To those whom God has chosen he has given them for an eternal possession. He has given them an inheritance in the lot of the holy ones. With the sons of heaven he has joined their assembly, for a council of community and a foundation of a holy building, for an eternal planting, for all the time to come.

As for me, I belong to wicked man, to the assembly of deceitful flesh. My iniquities, my transgression, my sin, with the perversion of my heart, belong to the assembly of worms and of those who walk in darkness.

For to a man is not his way, and a human being cannot direct his step. For to God is the judgment, and from his hand is the perfection of the way. By his knowledge all that is has come to be, and all that is he establishes by his thought. Apart from him nothing is done.

As for me, if I stumble, the kindnesses of God are my salvation forever. If I stagger in the iniquity of flesh, my justification is in the righteousness of God, which stands forever.

If he lets loose my distress, he will deliver my soul from the pit, and he will establish my steps in the way. In his mercies he has drawn me near, and in his kindnesses he will bring my justification. In his faithful righteousness he has justified me, and in the abundance of his goodness he will atone for all my iniquities. In his righteousness he will cleanse me from the uncleanness of man, and from the sin of the sons of man — to confess to God his righteousness, and to the Most High his glory.
