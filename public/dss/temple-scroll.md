# Temple Scroll (11Q19)

The longest Qumran scroll. A rewritten Torah of temple, purity, kingship, and festivals. English for reading in this app, following the surviving columns. Photographs: Leon Levy Digital Library.

## The covenant and the land

[…] You shall make a covenant with me, and I will be your God, and you shall be my people. I will dwell in the midst of the children of Israel forever.

You shall not make for yourselves molten gods. You shall not plant for yourselves an Asherah, any tree, beside my altar which you shall make. You shall not set up a standing stone which I hate.

When you come into the land which I give you, you shall not learn to do according to the abominations of those nations. There shall not be found among you one who makes his son or his daughter pass through the fire, a diviner, a soothsayer, a charmer, a sorcerer, one who casts a spell, one who inquires of a ghost or a familiar spirit, or one who seeks the dead. For all who do these are an abomination to me.

## The courts of the sanctuary

You shall make a house for my name, that I may dwell in the midst of the children of Israel forever.

You shall make a square court around the house, a court for the priests, one hundred cubits by one hundred cubits. The altar of burnt offering shall be in the midst of it.

You shall make a second court around the inner court, four hundred and eighty cubits by four hundred and eighty cubits, with twelve gates named for the sons of Israel: Simeon, Levi, Judah to the east; Joseph, Benjamin, Reuben […]

You shall make a third court around the middle court, one thousand six hundred cubits by one thousand six hundred cubits, for the congregation of Israel, for their wives and their children, and for the sojourners who sojourn among them.

No blind man shall enter it all his days, so that he may not defile the city in which I dwell. For I, YHWH, dwell in the midst of the children of Israel forever.

You shall make a fosse around the city, one hundred cubits wide, which shall separate the holy city from the city, so that they may not enter my sanctuary suddenly and defile it.

## The altar, the house, and the furnishings

You shall make the altar of burnt offering of stones, hollow, twenty cubits by twenty cubits, ten cubits high. You shall overlay it with bronze.

You shall make a house, sixty cubits long, twenty cubits wide, and thirty cubits high. You shall overlay it with gold, inside and out.

You shall make a veil of blue and purple and crimson and fine linen, with cherubim, the work of a skilful craftsman.

You shall make a table of acacia wood, overlaid with gold, for the bread of the presence, twelve loaves, two rows, six in a row.

You shall make a lampstand of pure gold. Its lamps shall burn always before me.

## The festivals (364-day year)

You shall keep the Sabbath, to sanctify it. Six days you shall labour, and on the seventh you shall rest.

On the fourteenth of the first month, between the evenings, you shall keep the Passover to YHWH. You shall sacrifice it before the evening offering, and you shall eat it at night in the courts of the sanctuary. All the males of the children of Israel, from twenty years old and upward, shall keep it.

On the twenty-sixth of the first month you shall bring the sheaf of the first fruits of your harvest to the priest. He shall wave the sheaf before YHWH. You shall count seven sabbaths complete from the day you bring the sheaf. You shall count fifty days, and you shall bring a new grain offering to YHWH, bread of the first fruits, on the fifteenth of the third month — the feast of Weeks.

You shall count from that day seven weeks seven times, forty-nine days, seven sabbaths complete, until the morrow of the seventh sabbath. You shall count fifty days, and you shall bring new wine for a drink offering, four hin from all the tribes of Israel — the feast of New Wine, on the third day of the fifth month.

You shall count from that day seven weeks seven times, and you shall bring new oil, a half-hin from each tribe — the feast of New Oil.

You shall offer the wood offering of the tribes, the feast of Wood, after the new oil.

On the first of the seventh month you shall have a memorial of blowing of trumpets.

On the tenth of the seventh month you shall afflict your souls. It is the Day of Atonement. You shall do no work.

On the fifteenth of the seventh month you shall keep the feast of Booths seven days. You shall rejoice before me.

On the twenty-second of the seventh month you shall have a solemn assembly, the extra day of the feast.

## Purity

You shall not eat the blood of any flesh. You shall pour it on the earth like water, and you shall cover it with dust.

You shall not eat an animal that dies of itself, or that is torn. You shall give it to the sojourner, or sell it to a foreigner.

When a woman conceives and bears a male, she shall be unclean seven days. Thirty-three days she shall remain in the blood of her purifying. For a female she shall be unclean two weeks, and sixty-six days in the blood of her purifying. She shall not touch any holy thing, nor come into the sanctuary, until the days of her purifying are complete.

The leper shall dwell alone. He shall dwell outside the camp. The priest shall look. If the plague is healed, he shall wash his clothes and shave his hair and bathe in water, and he shall be clean.

A man who has a flux shall be unclean. The bed on which he lies shall be unclean. Whoever touches it shall wash his clothes and bathe, and he shall be unclean until evening.

You shall make three places east of the city, separate from each other, where the lepers and those with a flux and the men who have an emission of semen shall go.

You shall not eat in the city any unclean flying thing, or the swarming things that creep on the earth.

## The king

When you come into the land which I give you, and you say, “I will set a king over me, like all the nations,” you shall set over you a king whom I shall choose. From among your brothers you shall set a king over you. You shall not set over you a foreigner, who is not your brother.

He shall not multiply horses for himself, and he shall not return the people to Egypt for the sake of horses. He shall not multiply wives for himself, lest they turn his heart from me. He shall not multiply silver and gold for himself exceedingly.

He shall write for himself this Torah, from the priests, on a scroll, before him. It shall be with him, and he shall read in it all the days of his life, that he may learn to fear me, to keep all the words of this Torah, and not to lift his heart above his brothers, and not to turn aside from the commandment, to the right or to the left — so that he may prolong days over his kingdom, he and his sons, in the midst of Israel.

He shall have twelve thousand men of war, a thousand from each tribe, with him, who shall not leave him. They shall be with him always, day and night. They shall guard him from every sinful thing, and from a foreign nation, lest he be captured.

He shall have a council of twelve princes of his people, and of twelve priests, and of twelve Levites, who shall sit with him together for judgment and for Torah. He shall not lift his heart above them, and he shall do nothing without them.

When he goes out to war, he shall not go until he has come before the high priest. He shall ask for him the judgment of the Urim. According to it he shall go out, and according to it he shall come in, he and all the children of Israel with him.

He shall not take a wife from the daughters of the nations. He shall take a wife from his father’s house, from his father’s family. He shall not take another wife in addition to her, for she alone shall be with him all the days of her life. If she dies, he shall take for himself another from his father’s house, from his family.

## Judges and witnesses

You shall appoint judges and officers in all your gates. They shall judge the people with righteous judgment. You shall not respect persons in judgment. You shall not take a bribe.

A single witness shall not arise against a man for any iniquity or for any sin. At the mouth of two witnesses, or at the mouth of three witnesses, a matter shall be established.

If a false witness arises against a man, the judges shall inquire. If the witness is a false witness, you shall do to him as he planned to do to his brother. You shall burn the evil from your midst.

## Idolatry and the prophet

If there arises in your midst a prophet or a dreamer of dreams, and he gives you a sign or a wonder, and the sign or the wonder comes to pass, and he says, “Let us go after other gods,” you shall not listen. That prophet shall be put to death, for he has spoken rebellion against YHWH your God.

If your brother, the son of your mother, or your son or your daughter, or the wife of your bosom, or your friend who is as your own soul, entices you secretly, saying, “Let us go and serve other gods,” you shall not consent. You shall not listen. Your eye shall not pity. You shall surely kill him. Your hand shall be first against him to put him to death.

If you hear in one of your cities that men, sons of Belial, have gone out from your midst and have drawn away the inhabitants of their city, saying, “Let us go and serve other gods,” you shall inquire and search and ask thoroughly. If it is true, you shall surely strike the inhabitants of that city with the edge of the sword. You shall devote it and all that is in it.

## Vows, war, and the beautiful captive

When you go out to battle against your enemies, and you see among the captives a woman beautiful in form, and you desire her, you shall bring her into your house. She shall shave her head and pare her nails. She shall put off the garment of her captivity. She shall remain in your house and weep for her father and her mother a full month. Afterward you may go in to her and be her husband. If you do not delight in her, you shall let her go where she will. You shall not sell her for money. You shall not treat her as a slave, because you have humbled her.

When you besiege a city many days, to fight against it to take it, you shall not destroy its trees, to wield an axe against them. Of them you shall eat. Only a tree which you know is not a tree for food, that you may destroy and cut down.

## Incest and sexual law

A man shall not take the wife of his father. A man shall not take the daughter of his father or the daughter of his mother. A man shall not take the sister of his father or the sister of his mother. A man shall not take the daughter of his son or the daughter of his daughter. A man shall not take the wife of his brother. A man shall not take a woman and her daughter. A man shall not take a woman and her sister as a rival, in her lifetime.

A man shall not lie with a male as one lies with a woman. It is an abomination. A man shall not lie with any animal.

## Conclusion of the Torah of the king and the people

You shall keep all my commandments which I command you today. You shall not add to them, and you shall not diminish from them.

I will be their God, and they shall be my people. I will dwell with them forever and ever.
