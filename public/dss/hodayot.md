# Thanksgiving Hymns (Hodayot)

**1QHᵃ**, with 1QHᵇ and 4Q427–432. Sectarian psalms of thanksgiving, often in the voice of a teacher. English for reading in this app. Photographs: Leon Levy Digital Library.

## I give you thanks, O Lord — the teacher’s portion

I give you thanks, O Lord, for you have placed my soul in the bundle of the living, and you have hedged me about against all the snares of the pit.

Violent men have sought my life, because I held fast to your covenant. They are a council of deceit, a congregation of Belial. They do not know that from you is my standing, and that in your kindness you save my life. For from you are my steps.

They have made me a reproach and a derision to the traitors. A counsel of truth you have set in my heart, and the waters of the covenant for those who seek it. You have shut the mouth of the young lions, whose teeth are like a sword, whose fangs are like a sharp spear.

For you, O my God, have hidden me from the sons of man, and your Torah you have hidden in me, until the time when your salvation is revealed to me.

## Clay and dust

I am a thing of clay, formed of water, a foundation of shame and a spring of uncleanness, a furnace of iniquity and a building of sin, a spirit of error and one perverted, without understanding, terrified by righteous judgments.

What shall I speak that is not known? What shall I declare that has not been told? All is engraved before you in a memorial inscription for all the appointed times of eternity, and the cycles of the number of years of all their appointed times, they are not hidden, and they are not lacking, from before you.

What is one born of woman, in all his great delights? He is a building of dust, kneaded with water. His foundation is sinful shame, a spring of uncleanness, a spirit of error. He is perverted without understanding, and he is terrified by righteous judgments.

What shall a man of nothingness recount of your glory? What shall he understand of your great wonder? And how shall he stand before your glory?

## You have opened my ears

I give you thanks, O Lord, for you have opened my ears to wonderful mysteries, and you have given understanding of your truth in my heart.

You have set me as a banner for the chosen of righteousness, and as an interpreter of knowledge in wonderful mysteries, to test the men of truth, and to try those who love discipline.

I have been a snare for offenders, but healing for all who turn from transgression; prudence for the simple, and a firm inclination for all whose heart is anxious.

You have made me an object of reproach and derision for traitors, a counsel of truth and understanding for the upright of way. All who have eaten my bread have lifted their heel against me. All who were joined to my counsel have mocked me with wicked lips.

But you, O my God, have helped the soul of the poor and the weak against one stronger than he. You have redeemed my soul from the hand of the mighty. You have not let me be dismayed by their taunts, so as to forsake your service for terror, or to change for good the statutes which you have engraved in my heart.

## The fountain

I give you thanks, O Lord, for you have placed me at a fountain of streams in a dry land, and at a spring of water in a land of drought, and at a watered garden.

You have planted a planting of cypress and elm and cedar together, for your glory; trees of life at a fountain of mystery, hidden in the midst of all the trees by the water. They shall cause a shoot to sprout for an eternal planting, to take root before they sprout.

Their roots reach to the deep, and their stock is open to the living waters. On the shoot of its leaves all the beasts of the forest shall feed. Its trunk shall be pasture for all who pass by, and its branches for all birds.

All the trees by the water are exalted over it, for they grow in their plantation, but they do not send out a root to the stream. The holy shoot is not revealed, with the plant of truth. They have not believed, and they have not considered.

But you, O God, have hedged its fruit with the mystery of mighty warriors and of holy spirits, and with a whirling flame of fire. No stranger shall come to the fountain of life, or drink the waters of holiness with the eternal trees, or make his fruit flourish with the planting of heaven. For he sees without recognizing, and he considers without believing in the fountain of life.

## From Sheol

I give you thanks, O Lord, for you have redeemed my soul from the pit, and from Sheol of Abaddon you have brought me up to an eternal height.

I walk about on a boundless plain. I know that there is hope for him whom you have formed from dust for an eternal council. The perverse spirit you have cleansed from great transgression, to stand in the standing with the host of the holy ones, and to enter into community with the congregation of the sons of heaven.

You have allotted to man an eternal lot with the spirits of knowledge, to praise your name in a common rejoicing, and to recount your wonders before all your works.

I, a thing of clay, what am I? Kneaded with water, for whom am I to be reckoned? What strength have I? For I have stood in the realm of wickedness, and with the wretched in a lot. The soul of the poor has dwelt with great tumults, and the disasters of misery have been with my steps.

When all the traps of the pit are opened, and all the snares of wickedness are spread, and the net of the wretched is upon the face of the water; when all the arrows of the pit fly without turning back, and they are shot without hope; when the measuring-line falls on judgment, and the lot of anger on the forsaken, and the outpouring of wrath on hypocrites, and the time of fury for all Belial; when the cords of death surround, without escape, then the torrents of Belial break forth on all who pass by, like a consuming fire in all their channels, to destroy every green tree and dry tree by their channels.

It rushes with sparks of flame, until there is no longer any of those who drink them. It eats down to the great deep. The torrents of Belial break into Abaddon, and the depths of the deep roar at the noise of those who cast up mire.

The earth cries out at the disaster which comes to pass in the world, and all its depths roar. All who are upon it go mad, and they melt in the great disaster. For God thunders with the tumult of his strength, and his holy dwelling resounds with his truth. The host of heaven give their voice, and the eternal foundations melt and shake. The war of the mighty of heaven rushes about in the world, and does not turn back until the determined destruction, forever. There is no like it.

## You have not forsaken

I give you thanks, O Lord, for you have not forsaken the orphan, and you have not despised the poor.

## The two spirits in the hymnist

I know, by the understanding that comes from you, that in your hand is the inclination of every spirit, and all its work you have established before you created it.

How can anyone change your words? You alone have created the righteous, and from the womb you have appointed him for the appointed time of good will, to keep him in your covenant, and to walk in all your ways, and to heap upon him in the abundance of your mercies, and to open all the distress of his soul to eternal salvation, and everlasting peace without lack.

You have raised his glory above flesh. But the wicked you have created for the time of your wrath, and from the womb you have set them apart for the day of slaughter. For they have walked in a way that is not good, and they have despised your covenant, and their soul has abhorred your statutes. They have taken no pleasure in all that you have commanded, and they have chosen what you hate.

For you have appointed them to do great judgments in the eyes of all your works, that they may be a sign for eternal generations, that all may know your glory and your great might.

What is flesh, that it should understand these things? How can dust and clay direct its steps? You have formed the spirit, and its work you have established. From you is the way of every living thing.

I know that no wealth can compare with your truth, and […] your holiness. I know that you have chosen them from all, and they shall serve you forever.

You will not take back your word. For you, O God of knowledge, have established all their deeds, and the judgment for all of them you have decreed, before you created them.

## Blessed are you, O Lord

Blessed are you, O Lord, God of compassion and abundant kindness, for you have made known to me these things, that I may recount your wonders, and that I may not keep silent day or night.

For your kindness to all, and your abundant compassion to all the sons of your good will. For you have made them know the counsel of your truth, and in your wonderful mysteries you have given them understanding.

For your glory you have cleansed man from transgression, to be holy for you from all abomination of uncleanness and guilt of unfaithfulness, to be united with the sons of your truth, and in a lot with your holy ones; to raise from the dust the worm of the dead to a secret of […] and from a perverse spirit to understanding of you, and to stand in the standing before you with the eternal host and the spirits of knowledge, to be renewed with all that is, and with those who know, in a common rejoicing.

Blessed are you, O God of compassion, who have given to the dust an eternal lot, and have purified the perverse spirit from great transgression, to stand in the standing with the host of the holy ones, and to enter into community with the congregation of the sons of heaven.

You have allotted to man an eternal lot with the spirits of knowledge, to praise your name in a common rejoicing.

## Closing

As for me, from you is the way. I have leaned on your kindnesses, and I have hoped for your abundant compassion. You have atoned for iniquity, and you have cleansed man from guilt, through your righteousness.

It is not for man. It is not for a son of man. The way is not for flesh. For to God is the judgment, and from his hand is the perfection of the way.

I will praise your name among those who fear you, with songs of thanksgiving and prayer, lying down and rising, and when I go out and come in. I will bless you, and I will recount your wonders, from season to season, at the coming of light for its dominion, at the turning of evening and the outgoing of light, at the beginning of the watches of darkness, and at the end of night, at the coming of day.
